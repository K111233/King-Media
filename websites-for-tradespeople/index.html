Questions

Straight answers.

What will it cost?

Your build price is £494.99 for 1 page, £899.99 for 2, £1,199.99 for 3, or £1,449.99 for 4 to 6, and it includes your first month. After that, the monthly fee is £49.99 for 1 or 2 pages on the 12-month plan (£59.99 for 3 or more), with a lower price on the 5-year plan. Or choose the Care plan at £19.99 a month for any size of site, with 1 change a month instead of 5. All prices include VAT.

Is the demo really free?

Yes, when we arrange it with you on a call after you send this form. If you’d rather skip the call and request a demo straight through our main website, there’s a £4.99 fee. Either way, there’s no obligation to go ahead.

I get my work from Facebook and word of mouth. Do I need a website?

That’s your call, and we won’t pretend otherwise. A website gives people who search for your trade somewhere to check your work, your area and your number, and it’s yours, whatever happens to your social media pages. See your demo first, then decide.

I don’t have a logo or professional photos. Is that a problem?

No. Send whatever you have. With your permission, we can also start from the photos and reviews already on your Google or Facebook page.

Is there a contract?

Yes, a clear agreement you read and sign online before we start. Plans have a 12-month minimum (or 5 years for a lower monthly price), then run month to month. If you leave early there’s an early exit fee, and the agreement shows exactly how it’s worked out. More questions answered

What if I need to change something later?

Text, WhatsApp or email us. Your monthly plan includes up to 5 changes a month (1 on the Care plan), such as new prices, photos or opening hours. New pages and new features are quoted first.
