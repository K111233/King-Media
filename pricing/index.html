The rules

How payment, changes and cancelling work.

The same plain rules for everyone. Your written order, or for websites your signed agreement, sets out the detail. A Business OS is set out in its own proposal.

Websites

  • Demo: £4.99, paid online, separate from your build price. Free if we arrange it with you on a call.
  • Build: paid by card when you sign your agreement. It includes your first month, and we aim to have your site live the next day.
  • Plans: 12-month minimum on the 12-month and Care plans; 5 years on the 5-year plan, in return for a lower price. Then month by month.
  • Changes: up to 5 a month (1 on the Care plan). Unused changes don’t carry over.

AI receptionist, enquiry setup and social media

  • Term: The AI receptionist, enquiry setup and social media are monthly, with no minimum term. Cancel with 30 days’ notice.
  • Payment: Setup fees are paid before we start. Monthly fees are paid by card, monthly in advance.
  • Allowances: we tell you at 80%, and never charge for extra use without your OK.
  • Setup refund: If we can’t make the AI receptionist or enquiry setup work with your phone or website, we’ll refund its setup fee.
  • Price changes: If a price changes, we’ll tell you at least 30 days before it affects you.
  • Support: Email support on weekdays, 9am to 5pm. We reply within one working day. Up to 2 changes a month to your wording, hours or settings, usually done within 2 working days.

Questions

Price questions.

Something not covered? Ask us. We reply within 24 hours.

Do your prices include VAT?

Yes. Prices include any VAT that applies. The price you see is the price you pay.

Why do some services cost more than others?

Because some take more of our time. The AI receptionist and enquiry setup run on software after we set them up, so the monthly price mostly covers the phone, text and software costs. Managed social media needs a person on your pages every week, so it’s priced by how much we do. A Business OS is built around your business, so it starts from a published price and is confirmed in a written proposal.

What happens if I use more than my allowance?

We tell you when you’ve used 80% of it. If you’re going to run out, you choose: buy a top-up for the rest of the month, or wait until it resets. We never charge for extra use without your OK.

Are there any other costs?

Not from us. A few things are paid to other companies directly, and we’ll tell you about them before you agree: for example, your phone network may charge for forwarded calls, and booking or payment platforms take their own fees. For a Business OS, any third-party software or usage costs are listed in your proposal.

How do I pay?

Websites: you pay the build price by card through Stripe when you sign your agreement, and Stripe takes your monthly payments. Our monthly services: the setup fee is paid before we start, then monthly by card, in advance. For a Business OS, payment is set out in your proposal.

Can I change or cancel?

The AI receptionist, enquiry setup and social media are monthly, with no minimum term: cancel with 30 days’ notice, or change plan from your next month. Website plans have a minimum term (12 months, or 5 years on the 5-year plan), set out in the agreement you sign. A Business OS is set out in its proposal.
