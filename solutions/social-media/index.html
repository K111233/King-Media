Managed plans

You send the photos. We do the rest.

A simple routine that keeps your pages active without taking your time.

  1. 01

    We set you up

    We tidy your profiles, agree your tone and topics, make branded templates, and get access through Meta Business Suite and Google, so you never share a password.

  2. 02

    You send photos as you go

    Snap the job, before and after, and send it on WhatsApp or upload it with a line of notes. That’s your part.

  3. 03

    We write and design

    Our Social Content Machine drafts posts from your real photos and notes, plus tips, seasonal ideas and reviews you’re happy to share. A person checks and edits every one before it’s scheduled.

  4. 04

    You approve the plan

    You see the posts before they go out, in one weekly or monthly preview. Prefer not to? You can choose to have them go ahead unless you object within 2 working days.

  5. 05

    We post and keep an eye on things

    Posts go out on your pages at sensible times. On Regular we check comments and messages 3 times a week and pass on anything that needs you. On Fully managed we answer them every weekday in your approved words.

  6. 06

    Monthly report

    What went out, and the reach and engagement figures your platforms show. No vanity promises.

Managed plans: included, and not

What you get, and what we won’t do.

You get

  • Posts from your real job photos, plus tips, seasonal ideas and reviews you’re happy to share, all checked by a person
  • Scheduled and published on your own pages
  • Branded templates and a consistent look
  • A plan you approve, and a monthly report
  • Comment and message checks on Regular and Fully managed, with enquiries passed to you

We won’t

  • Buy followers or likes, or promise reach, followers or enquiries
  • Post as your work anything you didn’t do: no stock photos passed off as yours
  • Make AI images of jobs, people or places
  • Quote, book or agree anything on your behalf. Enquiries are passed to you
  • Post anything you’ve asked us not to, or ignore your approvals

Questions

Social media questions.

Do I keep my accounts?

Yes. Your pages stay yours. We use Meta Business Suite partner access and Google Business Profile manager access, so you never share a password and can remove us at any time.

Who writes the posts?

Our Social Content Machine drafts them from your real photos and notes, and a person checks and edits every post before it’s scheduled. Nothing is posted without that check.

Do I have to approve everything?

You see every post in a weekly or monthly preview. If you’d rather not, you can choose to have posts go ahead unless you object within 2 working days.

Why does Fully managed cost more?

Because it takes far more of our time. Fully managed means being on your pages every weekday, answering comments and messages, and editing videos. Keep active is planned and scheduled once a week. You pay for the activity you need.

What’s the difference between the Social Content Machine and a managed plan?

The Social Content Machine (£29.99 a month) writes AI drafts from your photos and notes, up to 30 drafts a day, and you check, edit and post them yourself. On a managed plan, we do the posting for you: a person checks every post, schedules it on your pages and, on the higher plans, keeps an eye on comments and messages.

Does the Social Content Machine post for me?

No. It writes drafts for you to check and post. Nothing is published, scheduled or sent by it. The limit is up to 30 drafts a day; a rewrite counts as a new draft.

Can I have more posts, or a different mix?

Yes. Tell us how many posts you’d like, which channels and how often you want comments and messages checked, and we’ll send a clear quote within 24 hours, free. Extra posts on any plan are £10 each.

What if I don’t have many photos?

Phone photos are fine. Before-and-after shots work best. When things are quiet we use your approved back-up topics, and we’ll tell you if we need more material.

Can you promise more followers or enquiries?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that. What we promise is regular, professional posts that show your real work, done on time.

Do you run adverts?

Not as part of these plans. Paid adverts need a budget and more management, so we quote for them separately.

Can I change plans or stop?

Yes. Plans are monthly. Change tier from your next month, or cancel with 30 days’ notice. Your pages and posts stay yours.

Want your pages kept active?

Tell us about your business and the work you do. We’ll suggest the option that fits, or tell you if you don’t need one yet.
