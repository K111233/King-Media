Questions

Before you choose.

Something else? Ask us. We reply within 24 hours.

Can I use just one of these?

Yes. Every service works on its own, and you can add more later.

Do I need a website from you to use the other services?

No. Every service works on its own. If your website is with someone else, we can still set up enquiries, answer the calls you can’t take, help with your social media or refresh your Google profile.

How does setup work?

We agree the wording with you, connect the service to your phone, website or pages, and test it with made-up details before switching it on. You’ll get a date for that when you go ahead.

What do I have to do day to day?

Very little. The AI receptionist and enquiry setup run on their own and send you summaries. For social media, you either post the Social Content Machine’s drafts yourself, or send us photos of your work and approve the plan.

Which parts use AI?

The AI receptionist uses an AI voice to take messages, and the Social Content Machine uses AI to draft posts. Business OS may use AI tools where they suit a workflow, with important decisions left to you. Enquiry setup is a form and uses no AI. We’ll always tell you which is which.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The AI receptionist, enquiry setup and social media are monthly, with no minimum term. Cancel with 30 days’ notice. The Local Business Refresh is a one-off project, so there’s nothing to cancel.

Tell us what your business needs.

A website, an AI receptionist, help with your social media, or a connected Business OS. We’ll reply within 24 hours with honest advice and a clear price.
