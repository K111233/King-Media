Questions

Local Business Refresh: questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on your website and how much needs changing. We take a free look first, then give you the price in writing before anything starts.

Is it a monthly service?

No. It’s a one-off project. If you’d like ongoing help afterwards, our website plans and social media plans are monthly.

What do you need from me?

Links to your website and Google Business Profile, access to them (or help from whoever looks after them), and your current services, prices and areas, so the wording is accurate.

What goes in the quote-preparation guide?

We agree what it covers with you before we start, so it fits the way your business prices its work.

Has your website drifted out of date?

Send us your website and Google profile. We’ll take a free look and tell you what we’d change, with the price in writing.
