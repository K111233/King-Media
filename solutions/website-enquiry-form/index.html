How it works

What happens, step by step.

  1. 01

    We plan your questions

    Together we choose what to ask: the jobs you do, the areas you cover, and what you need to know before calling back.

  2. 02

    We add it to your website

    It goes on your existing site. No website? Ask us about a simple enquiry page you can link to from Google or Facebook.

  3. 03

    A customer fills it in

    Short steps, clear labels and helpful error messages, so it’s easy on a phone.

  4. 04

    They see a confirmation

    A thank-you message says you’ve got their enquiry. It doesn’t say you’ve taken the job or give a time.

  5. 05

    You get a summary

    An organised summary arrives by email, and by text if you like, with their details and the best time to call.

Included, and not

What you get, and what it won’t do.

You get

  • One enquiry form, designed for your business and added to your website
  • A summary of every enquiry by email, with optional text alerts
  • A confirmation for your customer that doesn’t overpromise
  • Spam protection
  • Small changes to the questions when you need them

It won’t

  • Chat with customers or answer their questions
  • Give prices, quotes or availability
  • Book jobs or take payments
  • Keep copies of enquiries on our server for longer than 90 days

Is it for you?

Who it suits.

Good for

  • Businesses with a website that gets messages by email or a basic contact form
  • Anyone who gets “how much for a quote?” with no details
  • Owners who want enquiries in one place, not lost in an inbox

Not the right fit for

  • Businesses that want a chatbot to answer questions. This is a form, not a chatbot
  • Online booking or taking payments

When it needs you

When it’s not straightforward.

Someone leaves out a detail
The form explains what’s missing and how to fix it, before anything is sent.
An email doesn’t arrive
We keep a short-term copy, so we can resend it to you.

Where you see it: An organised summary for every enquiry by email, plus a short text alert if you want one.

What we need from you.

  • Access to add the form to your website, or the details of whoever looks after it
  • The jobs you do and the areas you cover
  • Where summaries should go

Ask about setup

Questions

Website enquiry setup: questions

Is this a chatbot?

No. It’s a well-designed form that asks the right questions in a sensible order. It doesn’t answer questions or make anything up, which is what makes it dependable.

Will it work with my website?

We can add it to most website builders and WordPress sites, or give you a link to use instead. We check yours before setup.

What does my customer see after sending?

A thank-you message saying you’ve received their enquiry and will be in touch. It doesn’t say you’ve accepted the job or give a time.

Can people send photos?

Yes, if it helps you. Photos are optional, and we can include an upload for up to 3 pictures.

Where are enquiries kept?

They’re sent to you. Copies on our server are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Would enquiry setup suit you?

Tell us how you work. We’ll be straight with you about whether it would help, and what setup involves.
