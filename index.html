In writing

Promises, not small print.

Our website promises are written into the agreement you sign. Our other services come with the same plain rules.

  • Your demo, no strings.

    We aim to show you a real, working demo within 2 working days. Don’t love it? You owe us nothing more.

  • Live the day after you pay.

    That’s our aim, and it’s in writing. If your site isn’t live within 10 working days for reasons on our side, you can cancel and get your money back (apart from any demo fee).

  • Faults don’t use up your changes.

    If your site stops working, we start on it within one working day, and fixing it never counts as one of your changes.

  • No surprise bills.

    Your monthly website fee can’t go up during your agreement unless VAT changes or you accept a quote to add something. Anything extra is quoted first.

  • Your website, if you leave.

    Your content is always yours. Once everything’s paid, ask within 30 days and we’ll send you a copy of your website files to keep using for your business, and transfer your web address for no more than it costs us.

  • Covered if we can’t carry on.

    If we stop trading, or your site is down or your requests go unanswered for over 30 days, you can end the agreement with nothing more to pay. We’ll hand over your website and web address free.

  • No long tie-ins on services.

    The AI receptionist, enquiry setup and social media are monthly, with no minimum term. Cancel with 30 days’ notice.

  • Nothing extra without your OK.

    We tell you at 80% of any allowance, and we never charge for extra use unless you say yes.

  • If it can’t work, you don’t pay for setup.

    If we can’t make the AI receptionist or enquiry setup work with your phone or website, we’ll refund its setup fee.

Questions

Straight answers.

Can’t see yours? Ask us. We reply within 24 hours.

What does KWM Systems do?

We design, build and look after websites for local businesses, and set up the systems around them: an AI receptionist for the calls you can’t take, website enquiry setup, social media and a one-off Local Business Refresh. For businesses that want more, KWM Business OS connects selected tools and workflows into one system built around how you work.

Do I need a website from you to use the other services?

No. Every service works on its own. If your website is with someone else, we can still set up enquiries, answer the calls you can’t take, help with your social media or refresh your Google profile.

How are your prices worked out?

Websites have a one-off build price and a monthly fee. The AI receptionist and enquiry setup run on their own after we set them up, so they cost less each month. Managed social media takes our time every week, so it’s priced by how much we do. Business OS is tailored, so it has “from” prices and an exact quote in a written proposal.

Which parts use AI?

The AI receptionist uses an AI voice to take messages, and the Social Content Machine uses AI to draft posts. Business OS may use AI tools where they suit a workflow, with important decisions left to you. Enquiry setup is a form and uses no AI. We’ll always tell you which is which.

Will you promise me more work?

No. Nobody can honestly promise more enquiries, jobs or sales. What we do is make your business look professional, help people who try to reach you get a quick response, and pass their details to you.

Is there a contract?

Websites have a clear agreement you sign online (12-month, 5-year or Care plan). The AI receptionist, enquiry setup and social media are monthly with no minimum term, and you can cancel with 30 days’ notice. Business OS is set out in a written proposal and agreement.

I’m not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That’s exactly who we’re for. We set everything up, test it with you and look after it. Day to day, you just get summaries, enquiries and messages.

Who can see my customers’ details?

You can, and so can we, to run and support the service. We use a small number of suppliers for hosting, calls, messages and AI, under contracts that protect the data. We never sell it or use it for anything else.

Tell us what your business needs.

A website, an AI receptionist, help with your social media, or a connected Business OS. We’ll reply within 24 hours with honest advice and a clear price.
