Our promises

In writing, not in small print.

Each of these is written into our terms or the agreement you sign, so you can hold us to it.

  • Your demo, no strings.

    We aim to show you a real, working demo within 2 working days. Don’t love it? You owe us nothing more.

  • Live the day after you pay.

    That’s our aim, and it’s in writing. If your site isn’t live within 10 working days for reasons on our side, you can cancel and get your money back (apart from the demo fee).

  • Faults don’t use up your changes.

    If your site stops working, we start on it within one working day, and fixing it never counts as one of your changes.

  • No surprise bills.

    Your monthly fee can’t go up during your agreement unless VAT changes or you accept a quote to add something. Anything extra is quoted first.

  • Your website, if you leave.

    Your content is always yours. Once everything’s paid, ask within 30 days and we’ll send you a copy of your website files to keep using for your business. We’ll transfer your web address to you for no more than it costs us.

  • Covered if we can’t carry on.

    If we stop trading, or your site is down or your requests go unanswered for over 30 days, you can end the agreement with nothing more to pay. We’ll hand over your website and web address free.
