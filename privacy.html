1. Who we are

King Media is a sole trader based in the United Kingdom, at 124 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX. We decide how your personal information is used, which makes us the “controller” under UK data protection law. Contact us about anything on this page at enquiries@kingmedia.uk.

2. What we collect

  • When you request a demo: your name, business name, email address, phone number (if you give it), how many pages you think you need, what you need (including any booking, payment or shop details), your message, and any files you send us, such as your logo or photos.
  • When you ask a question: your name, email address and your question.
  • When you pay us: payments are handled by Stripe, a secure payment provider. We see that you’ve paid, but we never see or store your full card details.
  • When you sign your agreement online: the name and position you type to sign, the plan you choose, the date and time, your IP address and browser, and a copy of exactly what you signed. We keep these as a record of the contract.
  • If we contact you about our services: we may contact businesses whose details are publicly listed, for example on Google Maps. We use the business name, phone number, address, the contact’s name if it’s listed, and notes of our conversations.
  • When we work together: the emails and messages we exchange, and the content you ask us to put on your website.

We don’t use analytics or advertising trackers. Like every website, our host keeps standard server logs for security, and our forms briefly keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address to stop spam.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to you, build your demo and send you quotes. We do this because you’ve asked us to, as a step before a possible contract.
  • To build, host and look after your website. We need to do this to deliver the contract with you.
  • To keep business and accounting records, because the law requires it.

Some things we do because we have a legitimate interest in doing them: keeping a reliable record of who signed an agreement for a business and when, so we can show what was agreed and make or defend legal claims; protecting our website from spam and abuse; and contacting businesses about our services. You can object at any time. If you ask us not to contact you, we’ll stop and keep a note so we don’t contact you again.

We’ll never sell your information or use it for anything you wouldn’t expect.

4. Who we share it with

We only share your information with services we need to run the business, such as our website hosting, email and payment providers. Payments are handled by Stripe, and our email runs on Microsoft 365. These services may only use your information to provide their service to us. Some of them may process it outside the UK, with the safeguards UK data protection law requires. People who make sales calls for us see the contact details we need them to use. We don’t share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

When we host and look after a client’s website, we handle the personal information that website collects on our client’s behalf, as their “processor”. The client’s own privacy notice explains how they use it.

5. How long we keep it

If you start a demo request but don’t pay, it’s deleted automatically, usually within 2 days. Copies of requests and files on our website’s server are deleted automatically after about 90 days. If we don’t end up working together, we delete your enquiry and any files from our email within 12 months. If you sign an agreement but don’t pay, we delete the signed copy and its signing record after 12 months. If you become a customer, we keep your information, including your signed agreement and its signing record, for as long as we work together and then for 6 years after our agreement ends, for accounting and legal reasons. Then we delete it.

6. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit its use. Email us and we’ll respond within one month. If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk (opens in a new tab) or on 0303 123 1113.

7. Cookies and browser storage

This website doesn’t use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies, and it doesn’t load anything from third parties. Even our fonts are served from our own site. 8. Keeping it safe

Our website is served over a secure connection (the padlock in your browser). We only keep the information we need, and we limit who can see it.
