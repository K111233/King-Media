About Northside Barbers

A proper barbershop on the north side of town. Three chairs, good music and barbers who take their time over every cut.

Walk in and wait with a coffee, or book a chair and sit straight down. Either way, you leave looking sharp.

  • Walk-ins welcome all day
  • Kids welcome with an adult
  • Card and cash accepted
