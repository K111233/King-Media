Treatments, in plain English

No jargon. Here’s what we help with, and what a session involves.

  • Back and neck pain

    A stiff neck from the desk, or a back that twinges when you bend. We find the cause, ease it with hands-on treatment and show you simple exercises to keep it away.

    Good for: desk workers, drivers and new parents

  • Sports injuries

    Sprains, strains, a sore knee or a tight hamstring. We work out what went wrong, treat it and build a plan that gets you back to your sport without it happening again.

    Good for: runners, footballers and gym-goers

  • Recovery after surgery

    After a new hip or knee, or a repaired ligament or wrist. Step-by-step rehab that rebuilds strength and movement, at a pace that suits you.

    Good for: joint replacements, ligament repairs and fractures

Your first visit

Forty-five calm minutes, one to one. Wear something comfortable.

  1. We listen

    Tell us what hurts, when it started and what makes it worse. There’s no rush.

  2. We check and treat

    We look at how you move, explain what we find in plain words and start treatment the same day.

  3. You leave with a plan

    Clear next steps and a few exercises to do at home, sent to your phone.

About Halcyon Physio

A small, quiet clinic with three treatment rooms and time built into every appointment, so nobody feels rushed.

All our physiotherapists are registered with the Health and Care Professions Council, and we treat adults of every age and fitness level.
