Why choose us

  • Free, fixed quotes

    We come out, look properly and put one price in writing. No call-out fee.

  • Fully insured

    Public liability insurance on every job, with the certificate on request.

  • Guaranteed work

    Every job comes with a written guarantee. If it leaks, we come back.

What we do

Houses, extensions, garages and outbuildings. If it has a roof, we can look at it.

  • 01

    New roofs

    A full strip and re-roof in slate, tile or metal, with new battens, membrane and ridge.

  • 02

    Roof repairs

    Slipped or broken slates and tiles, leaks, storm damage and loose ridge tiles.

  • 03

    Flat roofs

    Rubber and fibreglass flat roofs for extensions, garages and dormers.

  • 04

    Guttering and fascias

    Gutters cleared, repaired or replaced, with new fascias and soffits to match.

Our work

How a free quote works

No pressure and no charge. You get a price you can hold us to.

  1. Tell us what’s wrong

    Send a short message, with a photo if you can. We reply the same day.

  2. We visit and measure

    At a time that suits you. We check the whole roof, not just the bit you can see.

  3. A fixed price in writing

    One clear price with everything included. It won’t change once we start.
