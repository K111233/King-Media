Lawn care, all year round

A lawn care plan means we visit on a regular schedule and do the right job for the season. You don’t have to remember a thing.

  • Spring Mar–May

    Scarify, feed and treat moss, then overseed any thin patches.

  • Summer Jun–Aug

    Regular mowing and edging, with advice on watering in dry spells.

  • Autumn Sep–Nov

    Aerate, top-dress and feed so the roots are strong for winter.

  • Winter Dec–Feb

    Leaf clearing, a tidy-up and a plan for next year.

Plans are priced after a garden visit, depending on the size of the lawn and how often you’d like us.

How a garden visit works

  1. Book a visit

    Choose a time that suits you. We’ll walk round the garden with you and listen to what you want from it.

  2. Get a written quote

    A clear, fixed price for the work, sent within a few days. No pressure and no hard sell.

  3. We get to work

    We turn up when we say we will, do the job properly and leave the garden tidier than we found it.
