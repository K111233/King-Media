About Wick & Wild

We pour every candle by hand, a few dozen at a time, in a small workshop. Soy wax burns cleaner and slower than paraffin, so an evening candle lasts for many evenings.

Our scents start on long walks: cut grass, woodsmoke, fig leaves after rain. If a scent doesn’t remind us of somewhere, it doesn’t make the collection.

Candle care

  1. On the first burn, let the wax melt right to the edge of the jar.
  2. Trim the wick to 5mm before you light it again.
  3. Never leave a lit candle unattended.
