About Crumb & Co.

A small neighbourhood bakery with one big oven. We start at 3am, bake in small batches and open the doors at 7. Everything is made here, by hand, from flour, water, salt and a lot of patience.

When the shelves are empty, we close for the day and start again tomorrow.

Celebration cakes
